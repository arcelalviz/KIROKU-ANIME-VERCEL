# Community, privacy and moderation checklist

KIROKU keeps watchlists and emails private and uses opt-in public handles/bios. Public reviews require moderator approval before appearing. Users can hide spoilers, like helpful reviews and report inappropriate content. Reports are reviewed in `/moderation` by accounts explicitly granted moderator privileges in Supabase SQL Editor.

For a public beta, create a posted community code of conduct (no harassment, hate, sexual content, piracy links, or sharing private contact details), establish a staffed moderation workflow, provide a way to contact moderators, document personal data retention/deletion and user rights, and conduct a privacy and security review. Do not enable unmoderated posting by changing RLS defaults or using service-role keys in the browser.

Be cautious with anime poster/cover rights, third-party metadata terms, and age-appropriate catalog filtering. Confirm suitability with rights holders and service terms before a large commercial launch.
